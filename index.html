04 / A little clarity

Good questions.
Straight answers.

A few things to know before we build together.

Can you help with an early-stage idea?

Yes. We can start with your audience, the problem you want to solve, and the essential features. From there, we shape a practical scope for a first release.

Can you improve an existing product?

We can discuss interface improvements, new features, infrastructure, and AI integrations. Share your current product and the challenges you want to address so we can identify a useful starting point.

How do you estimate cost and timelines?

Scope, technical complexity, and existing assets all affect a project. We first discuss your requirements, then agree on deliverables, milestones, and an estimate before development begins.

What should I include in my enquiry?

Tell us what you are building, who it is for, and what help you need. An approximate budget and target launch date are helpful if you have them, but an early idea is enough to start a conversation.
